Policymakers, therefore, need to interpret even this preliminary evidence of some 
US–China “decoupling” with extreme caution. Policy decisions made today to reduce 
economic interdependence between the two countries will have profound implications for 
both economies, and neither will escape unscathed.41 
The US-China trade and geopolitical relations will be complex in the foreseeable 
future. Currently, the US-China decoupling has a specific political and commercial 
momentum.42 Hopefully, these countries can construct a more stable balance 
between national security and economic interests. World peace depends on their 
success.
