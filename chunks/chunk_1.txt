Changes and Political Risk in the 
Global System – National Security,

China. This can be seen in the passage of new US legislation, recent US trade restrictions 
on exports and investment transactions with China, and worsening US relations with the 
World Trade Organization (WTO), particularly with its dispute resolution system. The Trump 
administration initiated a haphazard tariff and trade war with China, reversing decades of
