1. Introduction
There has been a tectonic change in trade relations between the United States (US) 
and China. This can be seen in the passage of new US legislation, recent US trade 
restrictions on exports and investment transactions with China, and worsening 
US relations with the WTO, particularly with its dispute resolution system. The 
following are seven observations concerning President Trump’s and President 
Biden’s trade policies.
