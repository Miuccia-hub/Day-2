1.1.1 The International Trade Organization
The negotiations for an international institution devoted to issues related
to international trade were not part of the Bretton Woods framework. As
Jackson notes, the evidence available does not support the conclusion that
an eventual ITO would be placed at an equal footing with the WB and
the IMF.10 March 1948 marked the successful conclusion of the negoti-
ations for an ITO Charter. However, due to a lack of agreement within
the US Congress, it never entered into force. After the United States in
1951 decided to no longer pursue Congressional approval of the Charter
for an ITO, the International Trade Organization was in fact ‘stillborn’.
