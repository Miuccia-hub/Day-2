international trade agreements have been identified. It has been argued
that the ‘avoidance of terms of trade losses’, ‘the establishment of
economic and political commitments against domestic interests’ and ‘the
pursuit of foreign-policy objectives related to peace and security’ may all
– to varying degrees – explain the existence of trade agreements such as
GA TT.20 These three approaches will be discussed in this subsection. It
should be noted, however, that the rationale behind the creation of the
multilateral trading system as such is, in most cases, different from the
motivation of individual states and customs territories to join the system
at a later stage. The rationale of their accession bids will be examined in
more detail in section 1.3.
