On 15 August 2024, Big-Mart presented a beneficiary certificate and accountant’s report 
to NAB, claiming payment of the sum of US$1.87 million . The beneficiary certificate stated that 
the Big Bluey wine “was not of merchantable quality in that it could not be sold because of 
consumer sentiment against it”. The accompanying accountants’ report calculated Big-Mart’s lost 
profits to be US$1.87 million. NAB is still scrutinizing the documents.
