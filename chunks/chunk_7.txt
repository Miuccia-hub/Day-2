The post-war world was anchored by the US and its belief that economic 
interdependence and multilateral institutions would benefit all nations and ultimately 
promote peace. This view changed under President Trump, who chose a narrower 
focus on national security and the US national interest. The narrative that free trade 
was in the overall interest of the US began to change, especially due to China’s 
increasing competition and confrontation. A new paradigm emerged, as the US 
policy began to focus more on confronting the consequences of China’s economic 
assertiveness and rising military capabilities.7
