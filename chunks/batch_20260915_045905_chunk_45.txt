be accepted by the WTO, this is associated with ‘significant increases in
growth and investment’. 51 More specific findings on the relationship
between WTO accession and foreign direct investment have been made
with regard to Viet Nam. In a case study, it was found that the country’s
WTO accession in 2006 has had a ‘strong and positive impact on
Vietnam’s imports and inward FDI [foreign direct investment]’. 52 It has
been observed that, more generally, FDI increases with GA TT/WTO
