1.3.4 Accession in Times of Regionalization and the Doha
Development Round
The negotiating arm of the WTO is often criticized for the lack of
progress that has been made during the Doha Development Round, which
was launched in 2001. Indeed, when put in contrast with the highly
effective and successful dispute settlement mechanism, the negotiating
function of the WTO may seem to be underperforming. In parallel with
the slow progress of multilateral negotiations, recent decades have seen a
proliferation of bi- and plurilateral liberalization initiatives, prompting
some to ask the question whether the WTO ‘has become passé?’ 76 In
