The Director-General of the WTO has recently defended global trade again, arguing 
that globalization needs to be reimagined.22 He notes that the post-war international 
economic architecture was based on economic interdependence. Now that this vision 
is under attack, an alternative narrative has evolved, asserting that countries need 
independence, not interdependence. This debate is still heated and, in many ways, 
fuels toxic debates over national security, industrial policy, and new protectionism.
